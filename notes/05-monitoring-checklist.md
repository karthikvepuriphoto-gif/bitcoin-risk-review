# Practical monitoring checklist (long MSTR)

**Not advice.** Risk factors and watches only. Position sizing is personal and should involve a fiduciary if needed.

## A. Bitcoin market & macro
- [ ] Spot BTC price vs your mental “stress” levels (e.g., −30/−50/−70%).
- [ ] Realized vol and options IV (deribit/ETF options).
- [ ] Futures basis / funding rates (overheating leverage).
- [ ] Spot ETF net flows (daily/weekly) — creation/redemption pressure.
- [ ] Miner revenue, hashrate, difficulty (selling pressure after halvings).
- [ ] USD liquidity proxies you already use (rates, DXY, risk assets).

## B. Protocol / security
- [ ] Bitcoin Core / major client security advisories.
- [ ] Contentious soft-fork or consensus debates.
- [ ] Pool hashrate distribution (HashrateIndex, mempool.space).
- [ ] Notable UTXO movements from ancient/P2PK clusters (confidence can move on headlines).

## C. Quantum / crypto migration
- [ ] Progress on BIP-360 / BIP-361 and mailing-list consensus.
- [ ] NIST / industry CRQC milestone claims (logical qubits, error correction) — ignore qubit marketing alone.
- [ ] Credible resource-estimate papers (update the “gap” annually).
- [ ] Whether Strategy / major custodians publish PQ migration plans.

## D. Strategy Inc (MSTR) specific
- [ ] Latest 8-K: BTC count, USD Reserve, USD Cash, ATM activity.
- [ ] Debt notional + preferred notional + annual int/div run-rate.
- [ ] mNAV / Net BPS (company dashboard) — premium compression risk.
- [ ] Amplification trend as BTC moves.
- [ ] STRC dividend rate decisions and preferred market stress.
- [ ] Any BTC sales under monetization program.
- [ ] Convertible notes: maturities, repurchase rights, refinancing windows.
- [ ] Dilution: shares issued YTD vs BTC gained per share (“BTC Yield” company metric — understand methodology).
- [ ] Index rebalance calendars if MSTR is in your benchmarks.
- [ ] Options open interest / gamma regime (short-term amplification).

## E. Custody (wherever BTC exposure lives)
- [ ] If self-custody: seed backups, passphrase, inheritance plan, firmware updates.
- [ ] If broker/held MSTR: brokerage concentration, margin flags (avoid unintended margin).
- [ ] No reuse of deposit addresses for on-chain BTC you control.

## F. Risk-factor checklist for position sizing (not prescriptions)
Factors people weigh when exposure is large:
- Drawdown survival: can you hold through MSTR −70% to −85% without forced selling?
- Correlation: MSTR ≈ high-β BTC; portfolio diversification may be lower than it looks.
- Liquidity needs: prefs/debt markets closing is a corporate risk; personal liquidity is yours.
- Horizon: financing flywheel needs time; quantum migration is multi-year.
- Alternative expression: spot BTC or spot ETF vs MSTR changes claim structure (see MSTR note).

## G. Hedges people use (descriptive — not recommendations)
| Approach | What it tries to do | Tradeoffs |
|----------|---------------------|-----------|
| Long-dated puts on MSTR | Cap equity crash | Costly; IV crush/expansion |
| Short BTC futures / inverse BTC products | Offset BTC factor | Basis, funding, tracking; tax |
| Replace some MSTR with spot BTC/ETF | Reduce corporate/dilution/mNAV risk | May reduce upside asymmetry from flywheel |
| Collar (buy put, sell call) | Bound range | Caps upside |
| Reduce leverage elsewhere | Survive vol | Opportunity cost |
| Preferreds (STR*) instead of common | Different claim in stack | Still BTC-linked credit; not “safe” |

None of these are endorsed here. Each has cost, basis risk, and behavioral risk.
